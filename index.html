FilterPhone · Mock build

One request.
Every supplier bids.

FilterPhone is a real-time broker between the people who need filters and the suppliers who sell them — both sides in a single build. The on-site customer speaks a request for quote (an RFQ). Suppliers with a vendor account see the requests they can actually service and bid. The customer picks a bid. Nobody bids on work they can't do: can't carry a line, can't reach the site, can't make the window — the request never reaches them, and the customer sees the reason instead of silence.

  1. The customer talks. Hands busy, so he speaks the RFQ: “Two cases of 20 by 25 by 1, MERV 8 — and one case of 16 by 20 by 4, MERV 13.” It transcribes and parses into lines.
  2. Point at the job. A saved job-site card and a delivery window, then the RFQ goes out.
  3. Vendors bid — the ones who can. Switch to the vendor side (top of the phone), pick a supplier, and bid on the live RFQ from your own price book, or decline with the reason attached. The other serviceable vendors' bids are simulated from their price books.
  4. Pick a bid, lock it by text. Bids rank by total price. The winner confirms by text with the ETA, the total, and a 6-digit code; the code goes in on a glove-sized keypad and the delivery is locked.

Demo data only. Every person, company, site, price, and stock count on this page is invented, vendor bids are simulated from that data except any bid you place yourself on the vendor side, and no order goes anywhere. Where the browser supports it, the mic uses real speech recognition and the read-back uses real speech synthesis; otherwise the mock's demo voice stands in so the flow can still be walked end to end.
