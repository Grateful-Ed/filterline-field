FilterLine · Mock build

One order.
Every supplier answers.

FilterLine is a third-party service with two kinds of accounts. Filter suppliers join as vendors. HVAC companies and their techs place orders. The platform's one job: take an order and find who can actually service it — the right filters in stock, deliverable to that job site, in that window — and among the suppliers who genuinely can, show the best price.

  1. Say it. The tech's hands are busy, so the app opens already knowing who he is and he talks: “Two cases of 20 by 25 by 1, MERV 8 — and one case of 16 by 20 by 4, MERV 13.” It transcribes and parses the order.
  2. Point at the job. Job-site delivery, specifically: a saved site card, a delivery window, a spoken drop-off note.
  3. The network answers. Every vendor is checked against the order. Can't carry a line, can't reach the site, can't make the window — they're out, with the reason shown. The ones who can are ranked by total price.
  4. Lock it by text. The winning vendor confirms by text with the ETA, the total, and a 6-digit code. The code goes in on a glove-sized keypad and the delivery is locked.

Demo data only. Every person, company, site, price, and stock count on this page is invented, vendor responses are simulated from that data, and no order goes anywhere. Where the browser supports it, the mic uses real speech recognition and the read-back uses real speech synthesis; otherwise the mock's demo voice stands in so the flow can still be walked end to end.
